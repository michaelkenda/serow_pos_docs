    Getting Started

    Welcome to Serow! This guide will walk you through the basics of getting up and running.

    Requirements

    hardware

    • computer
    • printer
    • internet cable (incase you might need a local server)

    software

    -windows 10,11(recommended) - Supported browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge,brave)
    - Internet connection
    - Business credentials / account

    Signing Up

    1. Go to Serow’s website
    2. Click Sign Up
    3. Enter your business name, email, password
    4. Verify your email

    Initial Setup

    • Add your first inventory item
    • Set your business details (currency, tax settings, etc.)
    • Set up alerts preferences (low stock thresholds, notification channels)

    Overview of the Dashboard

    • Total inventory value
    • Items low in stock
    • Recent sales
    • Alerts & notifications
